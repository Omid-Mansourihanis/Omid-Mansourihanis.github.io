Where is traffic congested, block by block?

Level of Service (LOS) for 131,507 census blocks in Chicago, Houston, Los Angeles and New York City, shown in 3D on OpenStreetMap and on Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles.

What the colours mean

LOSSpeed Performance Ratio (observed / free-flow speed)3-class
A> 85%Good

C55–70%Medium

F≤ 25%

Values are TomTom probe data for 2024, Monday 07:00–09:00, length-weighted over the street segments that bound or cross each block. Column height in the OSM view is the TomTom Congestion Index (1–6). Grey blocks had no probe observations; they are missing data, not free-flowing or congested blocks. Blocks are 2020 U.S. Census TIGER/Line geometry.
